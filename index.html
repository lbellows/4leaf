About Us

We offer free estimates to make sure you are happy with the price before work begins. Emergency service is also available for all our projects.

Based in the heartland of Upstate New York's Hudson Valley, we happily serve the following counties: Ulster, Orange, Dutchess, Rockland, Putnam, Westchester, Greene, and Delaware.

As the head electrician of 4 Leaf Electric for the last 14 years, I have worked in the fields of electric, electronics and general home redesign. I have a passion for making my customers happy and truly enjoy the work that I do.

My areas of expertise include: Residential and commercial electric, Club, studio, and live performance audio and lighting, Satellite systems, Home theater systems, Automotive audio, video, and lighting, Petroleum industry, Two-way radio communications, Global Positioning System, and Low voltage wiring.
